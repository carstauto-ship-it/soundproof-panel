Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit soundproof-panel.com.cn, contact us by email, WeChat or WhatsApp, or submit your details through one of our advertising lead forms (including forms hosted by Meta).

In short: we collect the details you send us so we can quote and supply acoustic panels. We do not sell your personal information. We use Meta's advertising tools to reach people who are interested in sourcing panels, and Meta processes some of that data on our behalf.

1. Who we are

DUMI Panel is the trading name under which we manufacture and supply soundproof, acoustic, wood slat and 3D wall panels ("we", "us", "our"). Our production facility is located in Nangong, Xingtai, Hebei Province, China. Our legal entity name, registered address and tax registration details are stated on our quotations, proforma invoices and commercial invoices, and can be obtained from the contact details in section 10.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, we are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. Our contact details are in section 10.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, email address, telephone or messaging handle, company name and country.
  • Enquiry details — the panel type, size, thickness, NRC rating, quantity, colour or finish, delivery port and any other specification you send us.
  • Correspondence — the content of your emails, WeChat and WhatsApp messages with us, including attached drawings, photographs and specification sheets.
  • Order and shipping details — delivery addresses, consignee details and documentation needed for export and shipping, where you place an order.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system and the pages you view on our site.
  • Advertising data — if you reach us through a Meta advertisement, Meta may provide us with aggregate information such as impressions, clicks and general location (for example, city level).

2.3 Information from advertising lead forms

When you complete a lead form in one of our advertisements, the form is hosted by Meta. The information you enter — typically your name, email address, country, business type and product interest — is passed to us by Meta so that we can respond to your enquiry. Meta's handling of that data is governed by Meta's own privacy policy in addition to this one.

We do not ask for, and do not want to receive, sensitive personal information (such as health data, religious or political beliefs, or biometric data) through our advertisements.

4. Cookies and advertising technologies

Our website itself is a static site and does not set advertising or analytics cookies. When you interact with one of our advertisements on Meta platforms, Meta may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies on its own properties to measure and optimise advertising delivery. That processing is controlled by Meta, not by us.

If we later add analytics or advertising pixels to this website, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. Who we share information with

We share personal information only as far as necessary, and never sell it. Recipients may include:

  • Advertising platforms — Meta Platforms, which hosts our lead forms and delivers our advertisements.
  • Logistics and freight providers — freight forwarders, carriers and customs brokers, to ship your order.
  • Payment and banking providers — banks and payment intermediaries, to receive payment.
  • Professional advisers — auditors, accountants and legal advisers, where necessary.
  • Authorities — where we are required to disclose information by law, regulation or a valid legal request.

6. International transfers

We are based in China and many of our customers are located outside China. Your information may therefore be transferred to, stored in and processed in countries other than your own, including China and the countries in which our service providers operate. Where required, we put appropriate safeguards in place for such transfers.

7. How long we keep information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an order — up to 24 months from our last contact, so we can pick the conversation up again.
  • Customer and order records — for the duration of the business relationship and thereafter as required by tax, customs and accounting law.
  • Advertising lead data — stored in Meta's lead management tools and in our own records, and deleted once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These include access controls on our business systems and limiting access to the members of our sales and finance teams who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
