Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is 50 sqm. The same minimum applies to custom sizes, colours and finishes.

How long does production and delivery take?

Stock panels are dispatched within 48 hours and custom panels take 7-15 days to produce. Door-to-door delivery is typically 30-45 days.

Can I order a sample before placing an order?

Yes. Sample kits are dispatched by air express and typically arrive in 3-5 days.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB Qingdao, CIF and DDP, shipping worldwide.

What certifications do the panels carry?

Panels meet CE, ISO 9001 and ASTM E84 fire safety standards.

How do I get a price?

Send your specs (panel type, size, thickness, NRC rating, quantity and delivery port) by email, WeChat or WhatsApp and you will receive a quote within 24 hours.
