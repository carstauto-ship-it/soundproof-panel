Why buyers stay

Factory, spec and paperwork

PET felt base bonded to an MDF timber slat, wrapped in real wood veneer or PVC veneer, with a single-side wrapped edge for continuous wall runs. Panels are delivered at 21 mm thickness in 2400×600, 2600×600 and 3000×600 mm standards, packed four per carton.

Certifications carried across the range: CE, ISO 9001, GRS, REACH and FSC. Special and 3D designs start at 300 sqm; standard custom orders at 100 sqm.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

100 sqm for custom orders; 300 sqm for special and 3D designs.

How fast will I receive a quotation?

Send size, thickness, NRC requirement and quantity — you get a quote within 24 hours.

Which certifications do your panels carry?

CE, ISO 9001, GRS, REACH and FSC.

What are the lead time and shipping terms?

Production 7-15 days; FOB Qingdao, CIF or DDP with 30-45 days door-to-door.

How many panel types can I choose from, and can you customise them?

204 acoustic and soundproof panels across 17 categories, produced in 12 grade levels; sizes, colours and veneers can be customised.

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Yes — sample kits ship before bulk production so you can approve the finish and NRC performance.
