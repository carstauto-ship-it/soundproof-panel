Frequently asked questions

204 acoustic & soundproof panels, factory direct — MOQ 100 sqm, quote within 24 hours.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is 100 sqm per order, including custom orders in non-standard sizes, colours or finishes.

How fast will I receive a quotation?

Send your specs — size, thickness, NRC rating, quantity and delivery port — and you receive a quote within 24 hours.

Which certifications do your panels carry?

Our panels meet CE, ISO 9001, GRS, REACH and FSC standards.

What are the lead time and shipping terms?

Production takes 7-15 days. We quote FOB Qingdao, CIF or DDP; door-to-door delivery runs 30-45 days.

How many panel types can I choose from, and can you customise them?

204 panels across 17 categories — wood slat, 3-sided wrapped wood, 3D acoustic wall panels and tiles, ceiling panels, desk and screen dividers, acoustic lighting, PET polyester, flexible panels and accessories. OEM / ODM customisation of size, colour and finish is available at MOQ 100 sqm.

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Yes — the Wood Slat Panel Sample Kit lets you check colour and finish before ordering.
