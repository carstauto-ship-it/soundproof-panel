Wholesale Acoustic Panels

Specifications, minimum order quantities, lead times and samples for B2B buyers.

DUMI Panel supplies polyester fibre acoustic panels wholesale from a 17,300 m² factory in Jinan, Shandong. Panels are manufactured by JiNan MingKai Decorative Technology Co., Ltd — our own factory — and marketed under the DUMI and DODOMI brands. We sell business-to-business to interior fit-out contractors, gym and fitness studio builders, recording studios, home-theatre and AV integrators, offices and schools.

Panel formats we supply

Hexagonal acoustic tiles, 9-square wall panels and self-adhesive fibre panels make up the core PET range. The same factory also produces wood slat acoustic panels, 3D textured wall panels and acoustic ceiling systems for projects that need a mixed specification.

Wholesale questions

Can I order 20 m² as a first trial?

Yes, for standard panel formats in stock colours. Custom sizes, custom colours and 3D profiles move to the 100 m² and 300 m² tiers because they need a dedicated production run.

Do you provide acoustic and fire test reports?

NRC, fire rating and formaldehyde reports are available on request for the specific panel you are specifying. We only send reports that match the material actually supplied.

Can installers cut the panels on site?

Yes. PET panels at 12 mm cut with a knife, which keeps site adjustments simple. Fixing is either adhesive or the impaler system, depending on the substrate.

What is the difference between the 12 grade levels?

Each panel family is produced in twelve grades, from Essential to Wholesale. Grades differ in felt density, colour consistency and edge finish; the panels page links to a specification sheet per grade.

Can you mix PET panels and wood slat in one shipment?

Yes. Combined pallets are normal for fit-out projects, and mixing formats does not change the MOQ of the largest item in the order.
