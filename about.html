About DUMI Panel

Since 2004, DUMI Panel (soundproof-panel.com.cn) has manufactured premium soundproof, acoustic, wood slat, and 3D decorative wall panels from our 1,000+ production line based in Jinan, Shandong, China. We specialize in noise reduction solutions for studios, theaters, hotels, schools, hospitals, offices, and residential applications.

Our Capabilities

  • 1,000+ production line · 100+ countries
  • CE / ISO 9001 / ASTM E84 certified
  • Polyester fiber, rockwool, fiberglass, wool felt, MDF, premium wood veneer
  • Acoustic foam, fabric wrapped, perforated, slatted, 3D cubic/wave/peak
  • Standard sizes 600x600mm, custom sizes available
  • NRC ratings 0.85 to 0.95
  • Class A fire rating on premium lines
  • OEM/ODM: custom colors, sizes, finishes

Industries We Serve

  • Recording studios, podcast rooms, broadcast facilities
  • Home theaters, media rooms, cinemas
  • Hotels, restaurants, retail spaces, offices
  • Schools, universities, lecture halls
  • Hospitals, clinics, wellness centers, spas
  • Churches, mosques, temples, auditoriums
  • Industrial facilities, warehouses, machine rooms
  • Residential homes, apartments, basements

Certifications

  • CE / EU compliance
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management
  • ASTM E84 Class A fire rating
  • EN 13501-1 fire classification
  • RoHS compliance for low-VOC products

Lead Times & Logistics

Stock panels ship within 48 hours. Custom orders complete in 7-15 days production + 30-45 days ocean freight (FOB Qingdao) or 7-10 days air freight (DDP via DHL/FedEx). Minimum order 50 sqm for standard panels, 100 sqm for custom.
